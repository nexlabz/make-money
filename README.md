# /make-money — Referral Business Launcher

**By [nexlabz](https://github.com/nexlabz)**

An AI skill that takes a complete beginner from zero to a live referral ("middleman") business. You bring customers, a vetted provider does the work, and you earn a referral fee in a strong currency.

## What it does
1. **Recommends 3 country + niche pairs** with strong currencies, well-paying niches and good seasonal timing.
2. **Suggests 3 business ideas** for your pick, with typical prices, referral fees and keywords.
3. **Writes a plain-English plan** and waits for you to reply "let's build".
4. **Drafts outreach**: where to find providers and customers, personalised messages, and a referral agreement.
5. **Builds the site**: a landing page, SEO pages and a form that feeds a Google Sheet.
6. **Automates lead piping**: routes leads to providers, follows up, chases payouts and sends weekly reports.
7. **Deploys everything**: GitHub, Vercel, your domain, Google Analytics and Search Console. You only sign in.

You don't need to know what GitHub, Vercel, DNS or SEO are. The AI does the technical work. You choose, sign in, and talk to people.

## Install

**Claude Code / Claude desktop:** copy `skills/make-money/` into your skills folder (for example `~/.claude/skills/make-money/`), then type `/make-money`.

**claude.ai:** zip the `make-money` folder and upload it under Settings → Capabilities → Skills.

**Any other AI (ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.):** paste the contents of `skills/make-money/SKILL.md` into the chat and say "Run this."

## Ground rules baked in
- Referral fees are income, not gifts. Record them properly.
- Providers disclose referral fees to their clients where that is expected.
- You never do regulated work (legal, tax, medical, financial advice). You only refer.
- No fake reviews, no impersonation, and no spam.
- The AI never types your passwords or asks you to paste secrets into chat.
